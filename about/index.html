About Jinxin Future

We build products that explore new possibilities created by AI.

Jinxin Future is the product brand of 瑾欣未来科技有限公司 (Jinxin Future Technology Co., Ltd.), a company focused on AI infrastructure and software-efficiency tooling. We work at the engineering edge of generative AI and large language models — building the underlying capabilities, the developer tools, and the real-life products that sit on top of them.

The company is led by Xiao Xin (肖欣), who holds a Master's degree from the University of Queensland and brings a combined background in computer science and marketing. That mix is deliberate: we care as much about whether a product is worth building as about whether it works.

We don't believe the interesting future of AI is more automation for its own sake. We believe it is a set of open questions — about expression, about relationships, about the real world — and that the best way to answer them is to build products people actually live with.

Our first product, ZIJI, is where our exploration of AI × Expression becomes something you can hold. More will follow, because the company is built to keep exploring what AI makes possible.
